Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Introduces CANTO, a transformer neural operator that maps directly from continuous CAD geometry to physical fields without meshing the input geometry.
  • Develops a theoretical framework for learning operators from geometric manifolds to function spaces of physical fields, representing geometry through sequences of parametric patches.
  • Tokenizes non-uniform rational B-spline (NURBS) patches directly from control points, knot vectors, and weights, and predicts continuous surface and volume fields at arbitrary query locations.
  • Enables gradient-based inverse design through differentiability with respect to CAD parameters.

Methodology

CANTO is a transformer neural operator that tokenizes NURBS patches from control points, knot vectors, and weights and maps them to continuous surface and volume fields at arbitrary query locations. It is evaluated on four automotive and aircraft aerodynamics benchmarks: AhmedML, WindsorML, DrivAerML, and HiLiftAeroML, using surface-pressure relative L2 error and comparisons to AB-UPT. Gradient-based inverse design is demonstrated on AhmedML under volume and lift constraints, with results verified using the same CFD setup used to generate the dataset.

Key Results

CANTO achieves state-of-the-art accuracy on most evaluated surface and volume prediction tasks, including a 19.8% reduction in surface-pressure relative L2 error compared with AB-UPT on HiLiftAeroML. On AhmedML inverse design, CANTO identifies designs with 4.4 to 20.4% lower drag than the best dataset designs satisfying the same volume and lift constraints.

Limitations

  • The abstract reports state-of-the-art accuracy on most, but not all, evaluated surface and volume prediction tasks, indicating the improvement is not uniform across all benchmarks/tasks.
  • No other explicit limitations are stated in the title or abstract.
